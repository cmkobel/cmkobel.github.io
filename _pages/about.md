I'm a bioinformatician in multi-omics integration seeking to distill biological interpretations of multi-modal large-scale experimental data. I use dimensionality reduction and deep learning to identify concrete biological factors from noisy datasets. Meta-omics (microbes) often play a large role in my work, and I currently work with spatial transcriptomics.
